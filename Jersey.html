JERSEY

Welcome to my photo library of Jersey.

Jersey is the largest of the Channel Islands, covering about 120 km² (45.5 square miles). It lies in the Bay of St Malo, approximately 22 km from the French coast and 137 km south of England. The island is around 14.5 km wide and 8 km long and is divided into 12 parishes, with St Helier as its capital. Jersey had an estimated population of 103,267 in the 2021 census.
